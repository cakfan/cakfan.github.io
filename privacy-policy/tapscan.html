TapScan Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Taufan Fatahillah ("Developer") handles data when you use TapScan ("the app"), an Android PDF document scanner published on Google Play Store.

2. Data We Collect

TapScan does not require an account and does not send your documents to the Developer. The app does not include third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

  • Scanned images, generated PDFs, thumbnails, and extracted OCR text are stored locally on your device.
  • App preferences, such as your selected view mode and batch-scan setting, are stored locally on your device.
  • Pro entitlement status is cached locally so the app can confirm your purchase when you reopen it.
  • The Developer does not operate a server and does not receive your document content, OCR text, or personal information.

3. Camera and Document Processing

  • The camera is used only when you start a scan, through the document scanner provided by Google Play services.
  • Document detection, image processing, and OCR run on your device. Your document images and recognized text are not sent to the Developer.
  • Google may collect usage and diagnostic data while providing these services, as described in the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Sharing and Exports

  • TapScan shares or exports files only when you choose to do so through the Android share sheet or the system file picker.
  • If you export to Google Drive or another provider, that provider receives the file you selected and handles it under its own privacy policy.
  • TapScan does not access your Google Drive account directly and does not store credentials for third-party storage services.

5. Purchases

  • TapScan Pro is a one-time purchase processed by Google Play Billing.
  • Google handles payment information. The Developer does not receive your card number or full billing details.
  • The app queries Google Play for your purchase status and stores the result locally.

6. Advertising

  • The free version of TapScan may display a single banner ad on the Home screen, served by Google AdMob. Pro users do not see ads.
  • AdMob may collect device information, your advertising identifier, and app usage data to serve and measure ads.
  • Where required, consent for personalized advertising is requested through Google's User Messaging Platform before personalized ads are shown.
  • Ad processing is subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

8. Data Storage and Retention

  • Documents and app data remain on your device until you delete them in the app or uninstall TapScan.
  • The Developer has no server-side copy of your documents and cannot access or delete them for you.
  • If you have Android backup enabled, your device may include app data in its backup according to your device and Google account settings.

9. Children's Privacy

  • TapScan is not directed to children under 13.
  • The Developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

  • This policy may be updated from time to time.
  • Changes are posted on this page with an updated date.
  • Continued use of TapScan after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
